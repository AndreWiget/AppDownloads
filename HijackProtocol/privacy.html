🇬🇧 English - Privacy Policy

Wiget Informatik GmbH ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use our mobile game Hijack Protocol: Cyber Tactic (the "Game").

1. Information Collection and Storage

  • Local Game Data: Hijack Protocol stores your gameplay progress, sector clearances, unlocked robot chassis, daily hacker scores, credits, and custom settings locally on your device. We do not transmit or store personal gameplay progress on external servers.
  • No Personal Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide personal information (such as your name, email address, or phone number) to play the game. You can choose a local hacker callsign, which stays stored on your device.

2. Advertising and Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

Hijack Protocol uses Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC) to serve banner, interstitial, and rewarded video advertisements ("Neural Sponsor") to support the development and maintenance of the Game.
Google AdMob may collect and process certain data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy, including:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID / GAID)
  • IP address and coarse location data
  • App usage and advertising performance metrics

For more information on how Google collects and uses your data, please visit:

  • Google's Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

3. Device Permissions

The Game requests minimal permissions strictly necessary for its operation:

  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to load daily infiltration parameters and serve advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • VIBRATE: Used solely for in-game tactile and haptic feedback during robot movement, laser attacks, and hacking steps (can be toggled on/off in the Config menu).

4. Children’s Privacy

Hijack Protocol does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

  • Company: Wiget Informatik GmbH
  • Website / Contact: Wiget Informatik GmbH
