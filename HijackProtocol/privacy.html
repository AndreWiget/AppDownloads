🇬🇧 English - Privacy Policy

Wiget Informatik GmbH ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use our mobile game Hijack Protocol: Cyber Tactic (the "Game").

1. Information Collection and Storage

  • Local Game Data: Hijack Protocol stores your gameplay progress, sector clearances, unlocked robot chassis, daily hacker scores, credits, and custom settings locally on your device. We do not operate our own servers and do not receive your gameplay progress.
  • Google Play Games (optional): If you sign in with Google Play Games, your progress is additionally kept in your Play Games cloud save so you can continue on another device. Achievements and leaderboard scores (Daily Infiltration, Endless Infiltration) are sent to Google Play Games and shown with your Play Games profile name. This data is processed by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; we do not receive it. You can play without signing in.
  • No Personal Account Required: You do not need to register with us or provide personal information (such as your name, email address, or phone number) to play the game. You can choose a local hacker callsign, which stays stored on your device.

2. Advertising and Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

Hijack Protocol uses Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC) to serve banner, interstitial, and rewarded video advertisements ("Neural Sponsor") to support the development and maintenance of the Game.
Google AdMob may collect and process certain data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy, including:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID / GAID)
  • IP address and coarse location data
  • App usage and advertising performance metrics

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before ads are loaded (Google User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice at any time under "Ad privacy settings" in the Config menu.

For more information on how Google collects and uses your data, please visit:

  • Google's Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

3. In-App Purchase ("No Ads")

You can remove banner ads with a one-time purchase. The payment is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; we only learn from Google Play whether the purchase is active.

4. Device Permissions

The Game requests minimal permissions strictly necessary for its operation:

  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to load mission packs, to connect to Google Play Games and Google Play Billing, and to serve advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • VIBRATE: Used solely for in-game tactile and haptic feedback during robot movement, laser attacks, and hacking steps (can be toggled on/off in the Config menu).

5. Children’s Privacy

Hijack Protocol does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

  • Company: Wiget Informatik GmbH
  • Website / Contact: Wiget Informatik GmbH
